Problem Statement

The Automotive Sourcing Bottleneck

Global Vulnerability

Automotive supply chains are highly vulnerable to disruptions (geopolitical tensions, microchip allocation caps, shipping constraints, and sudden raw material halts). Single points of failure threaten production continuity and trigger massive cost escalations.

The Sourcing Challenge

To prevent shutdowns, manufacturers require an agile solution that proactively monitors supplier risk, analyzes complex unstructured quality signals, and executes **multi-criteria alternate sourcing** that balances Cost, Capacity, and Geopolitical Risk.

The Solution

AutoSphere AI Prototype

Core Engine — Stage 1

Demand Forecasting & Verification

AutoSphere AI predicts exact monthly quantities using a Least-Squares Linear Regression model ($y = m \cdot t + c$).

If sudden market crashes result in negative projected slopes, the engine activates a robust fallback: it shifts to a **Simple Moving Average (SMA)** of the last 3 periods, ensuring the floor never goes below 0.

# Linear Fit Equation Solver:
m, c = np.linalg.lstsq(A, y, rcond=None)[0]
forecast = m * (n + 1) + c

Core Engine — Stage 2

Unstructured Feedback & NLP Risk Parser

Dynamic Entity Extraction

AutoSphere scans text fields using case-insensitive boundary regexes to dynamically link suppliers (e.g. *Apex Parts Corp*) and parts (e.g. *Brake Pads*).

Keyword Severity Rules:

  • Critical (+0.30) recall, hazard, dangerous, crack, fire
  • Severe (+0.15) fail, defect, faulty, leak, broken
  • Moderate (+0.08) wear, delay, slow, rust, friction

During simulation, raw feedback triggers dynamic updates that are instantly written to the persistent JSON layer:

Core Engine — Stage 3

Multi-Criteria Procurement Optimizer

Sourcing decisions can't rely on raw price alone. AutoSphere AI normalizes data parameters using **Min-Max Scaling** to rank candidates fairly:

  • Cost Score (CS): Inverted (lower cost = 1.0, max cost = 0.0)
  • Risk Score (RS): Inverted (1.0 - risk_score)
  • Capacity Score (CapS): Direct (max capacity = 1.0, min = 0.0)
Composite Optimization Formula:
Score = 0.40 * CS + 0.40 * RS + 0.20 * CapS

Why did S2 (Vertex Automotive) win?

• **Vertex (S2)** had the highest base cost (**$48.00**), yielding a Cost Score of **0.00**.

• However, due to its low risk (**0.08**) and maximum capacity (**12,000**), it achieved a Risk Score of **0.92** and Capacity Score of **1.00**.

• Balanced Utility: Score = 0.5680 (winning Rank #1 over compromised low-cost alternatives!).
